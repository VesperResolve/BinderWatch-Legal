Free Windows Desktop App

Track your cards.

BinderWatch is a lightweight MTG price watchlist widget for Windows. Set target prices, get notified when cards hit them, and buy with one click.

Download for Windows
What it does
A smarter way to watch card prices

Live price tracking

Real-time market prices from TCGPlayer and Card Kingdom, pulled directly through the Scryfall API.

Target price alerts

Set a target price per card. BinderWatch notifies you the moment a card drops to your number.

One-click buying

Click any price chip to jump straight to the TCGPlayer or Card Kingdom listing and complete your purchase.

Full card details

Click any card to see artwork, set info, oracle text, and a full price breakdown across both marketplaces.

Always-on-top widget

Lightweight and stays out of your way. Pin it above any window and monitor your watchlist while you play.

Windows native

Built with Electron for a clean desktop experience. Runs quietly in the background and starts with Windows.
