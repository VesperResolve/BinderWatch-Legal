# Privacy Policy for BinderWatch

**Last Updated: June 3, 2026**

## Overview

BinderWatch is a free Magic: The Gathering card price tracking widget developed by an independent creator. This Privacy Policy explains how BinderWatch handles your data.

---

## Data We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

BinderWatch stores the following data **locally on your device only**, in a file saved to your computer's application data folder:

- Card names and set information you choose to track
- Target prices you set for your cards
- Window position preferences

This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

### Data We Do NOT Collect

BinderWatch does **not** collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, or any personal identifying information
- Your Magic: The Gathering collection details beyond what you manually add
- Your location
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Payment information of any kind

---

## Third-Party Services

BinderWatch communicates with the following third-party services to function:

### Scryfall API
Card data and card images are retrieved from the [Scryfall API](https://scryfall.com/docs/api). When BinderWatch fetches card information, your device makes a request to Scryfall's servers. Please review [Scryfall's Privacy Policy](https://scryfall.com/docs/privacy) for details on how they handle requests.

### Card Kingdom
Current buy pricing is retrieved from Card Kingdom's publicly available price list. BinderWatch accesses this data to display prices within the app.

### TCGPlayer
BinderWatch displays links to TCGPlayer product pages. Clicking these links will take you to TCGPlayer's website, which has its own privacy policy and terms of service.

---

## Advertising

BinderWatch is supported by ads. Ad networks may collect certain device-level information to serve relevant advertisements, including:

- Device type and operating system
- A randomly assigned advertising identifier
- General location (city/region level, based on IP address)

We do not share your card watchlist or any personal data with advertisers.

For details on how the ad network handles data, please refer to the privacy policy of the ad provider integrated into the version of BinderWatch you are using.

---

## Children's Privacy

BinderWatch is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

---

## Data Security

Since all user data is stored locally on your device, the security of that data depends on the security of your device. We do not have access to your watchlist data.
